# The Umbrella That Sues the Clouds

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a` — a program that
writes one sentence pitching a ridiculous invention to `/output/entry.txt`.

Bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a

## The pitch

> Meet the umbrella that sues the clouds for emotional damages every time it rains, then sends the sky an itemised bill for one pair of soggy socks.

## Why this pitch

The judge's brief asks for a ridiculous invention fixing a tiny everyday
annoyance, judged on funniness, cleverness and originality. This one names a
fresh absurdity — an umbrella that takes the weather to court — and the laugh
comes from the invention itself: suing the clouds, then billing the sky for
one pair of soggy socks. The mechanism is silly yet weirdly plausible, and
the sentence stays tight at 153 characters.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the pitch above, exactly, to `/output/entry.txt`
— 153 Unicode code points, no network, fully deterministic: two runs produce
byte-identical output.
